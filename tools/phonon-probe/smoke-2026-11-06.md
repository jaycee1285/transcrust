## Confirmed integration fault

The root only started Phonon after microphone release. Its internal loop was
stream-capable, but all audio had already been buffered. The submitted take
therefore paid 9.52 seconds of ASR plus 1.67 seconds of decisions after release
(11.20 seconds to typing). The old “processing before release” log label was
incorrect: it described work before draining the receiver, not actual release.

The fix starts Phonon on microphone press, tees original chunks immediately to
its worker and retains an exact corpus copy. Delivery and decisions still wait
for release; model/window/decoding/glossary policy are unchanged. Other engines
retain their existing dispatch. Tests check forwarding before the sender closes,
exact banking, and banking behavior when the engine receiver fails.
